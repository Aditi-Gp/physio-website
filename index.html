Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, simple answers about online telephysiotherapy care.

You connect with Dr. Anchal via a secure, private 1-on-1 video call. She assesses your movement, posture, and symptoms in real time, guides you through corrective exercises, and provides a customized home recovery plan.
Vestibular disorders (vertigo & dizziness), neck and back pain, neurological conditions, sports injuries, post-surgical rehabilitation, and ergonomic posture strain are all effectively treated via telephysiotherapy.
All you need is a smartphone, tablet, or laptop with a working camera, a stable internet connection, comfortable clothing to move in, and a small space for exercises. You can also share any past reports.
Initial consultations are ₹1,500 and follow-ups are ₹1,000. We accept UPI, GPay, PhonePe, and major cards for domestic patients, and PayPal & Stripe for international patients.
Most patients notice meaningful pain reduction and mobility improvements within 2 to 4 sessions when consistently performing their prescribed rehabilitation exercises.
