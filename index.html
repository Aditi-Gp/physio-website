Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about online consultations and treatment procedures.

How do online physiotherapy consultations work over video?

Online video consultations are conducted via Google Meet, Zoom, or WhatsApp Video. Dr. Anchal conducts a thorough visual assessment of your posture, movement range, and functional limitations. You will receive real-time corrective feedback, guided therapeutic exercises, and a structured recovery plan sent directly to your phone.

Is online physiotherapy effective for severe or chronic pain?

Yes! Numerous clinical studies demonstrate that supervised exercise therapy, postural re-education, and ergonomic management provide equivalent long-term clinical outcomes to hands-on sessions for most musculoskeletal and spinal conditions.

What equipment or space do I need before our video session?

You only need a smartphone, tablet, or laptop with a working camera, a stable internet connection, comfortable clothing that allows free movement, and a small open floor space (or yoga mat) with good room lighting.

How many sessions will I need before seeing noticeable improvement?

Most patients notice relief within 2 to 4 sessions when consistently performing their customized home exercises. During the initial consultation, Dr. Anchal provides an honest prognosis and estimated timeline based on your specific condition.

Can I share previous doctor prescriptions, X-rays, or MRI reports?

Absolutely. You can upload your previous investigations directly through the booking form or share PDF/images over WhatsApp before your session so Dr. Anchal can review your full medical history.

What payment methods are supported for domestic & international patients?

Domestic patients within India can pay via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), NetBanking, and Credit/Debit cards via Razorpay. International patients can make seamless payments in USD/GBP/EUR via PayPal.
